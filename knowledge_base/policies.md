# Business Policies

**Revisions:** Each package includes a limited number of revision rounds,
specified per package. Additional revisions are billed hourly.

**Payment terms:** 50% deposit to begin work, 50% on delivery. For
Enterprise packages, milestone-based payments can be arranged.

**Support:** Post-delivery support windows are listed per package.
Extended support/maintenance is available as a separate monthly retainer.

**Cancellation:** Clients may cancel before work begins for a full refund
of the deposit minus any work already completed.

**Confidentiality:** All client information and project details are kept
confidential and are not shared with third parties without consent.
