# Pricing & Packages

**Starter Package — $1,500**
A single-feature AI integration (e.g. one chatbot or one automation
workflow). Includes 2 weeks of support after delivery.

**Standard Package — $4,000**
A full AI voice receptionist or RAG chatbot system with a basic dashboard.
Includes database setup, 3 revision rounds, and 30 days of support.

**Custom / Enterprise Package — starting at $8,000**
Full custom systems combining voice AI, RAG, workflow automation, and a
production dashboard. Pricing depends on scope — a discovery call is
required before a final quote is given.

All prices are in USD and exclude third-party API costs (e.g. OpenAI,
Vapi, hosting), which are billed to the client directly or passed through
at cost.
