# Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does a typical project take?**
Starter packages typically take 1-2 weeks. Standard packages take 3-5
weeks. Enterprise/custom projects vary based on scope and are estimated
after a discovery call.

**Do you offer ongoing maintenance?**
Yes, as a separate monthly retainer after the initial project is
delivered.

**Can I speak with a developer before committing?**
Yes — we're happy to schedule a call with a developer to discuss your
project requirements before you commit to a package.

**What technologies do you use?**
Primarily Python (FastAPI), React, LangGraph, Supabase (PostgreSQL +
pgvector), n8n for automation, and Vapi for voice AI.
